# Pivotal Flow Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Pivotal Flow! This guide will help you get started with the platform quickly and efficiently.

## Getting Started

### **Step 1: Access the Platform**

1. **Navigate to Pivotal Flow** in your web browser
2. **Enter your credentials** provided by your administrator
3. **Click "Sign In"** to access your dashboard

### **Step 2: Complete Your Profile**

1. **Click on your profile** in the top-right corner
2. **Update your information** including name, email, and preferences
3. **Set your time zone** for accurate time tracking
4. **Upload a profile picture** (optional)

### **Step 3: Explore the Dashboard**

The dashboard provides an overview of your key metrics:
- **Active Quotes**: Current quotes in progress
- **Pending Invoices**: Invoices awaiting payment
- **Time This Week**: Hours logged this week
- **Recent Activity**: Latest system activity

## Essential Workflows

### **Creating Your First Quote**

1. **Navigate to Quotes** from the main menu
2. **Click "Create New Quote"**
3. **Select a Customer** from your customer list
4. **Add Line Items** with descriptions and pricing
5. **Review and Send** the quote to your customer

### **Logging Time**

1. **Go to Time Tracking** from the main menu
2. **Click "Log Time"**
3. **Select Project and Task** from the dropdown
4. **Enter Time Details** including hours and description
5. **Submit for Approval** (if required)

### **Managing Customers**

1. **Access Customer Management** from the main menu
2. **Click "Add New Customer"**
3. **Enter Customer Information** including contact details
4. **Save Customer Profile** for future reference
5. **View Customer History** for all interactions

### **Generating Invoices**

1. **Navigate to Invoices** from the main menu
2. **Click "Create Invoice"**
3. **Select from Quote** or create new invoice
4. **Review Invoice Details** and pricing
5. **Send Invoice** to customer
